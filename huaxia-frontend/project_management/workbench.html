Source Text (原文)

皮影戏，又称“影子戏”或“灯影戏”，是一种以兽皮或纸板做成的人物剪影以表演故事的民间戏剧。

表演时，艺人们在白色幕布后面，一边操纵影人，一边用当地流行的曲调讲述故事，同时配以打击乐器和弦乐，有浓厚的乡土气息。

译员备注：这里的“乡土气息”建议意译，不要直译为 earth smell。

Target / Review (译文/审校)

Shadow play, also known as "shadow puppetry" or "lamp shadow show", is a kind of folk drama that performs stories with silhouettes of figures made of animal skin or cardboard.

During the performance, artists are behind the white curtain, manipulating the shadow figures while telling stories in popular local tunes, accompanied by percussion and string instruments, featuring a strong local flavor.
